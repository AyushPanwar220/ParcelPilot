Cancellation & Service Credit SOP v4 (Section 1):
- DRAFT orders: INR 0
- BOOKED, cancelled within 30 min: INR 0
- BOOKED, cancelled after 30 min: INR 250

Section 2: Customers qualify for a service credit if pickup is more than 2 hours late due to carrier fault. The default credit is the lower of INR 500 or 10% of the shipment fee.
